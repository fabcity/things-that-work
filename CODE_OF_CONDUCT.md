# Code of Conduct

This project serves people in crisis. We hold contributors to a simple standard:
**be respectful, be helpful, do no harm.**

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Harassment, discrimination, or sharing dangerous or knowingly false survival
information is not tolerated. Content that could endanger people (fabricated figures,
unsafe medical instructions) will be removed.

Report concerns to **tomas@fab.city**.
