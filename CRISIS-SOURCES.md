# Crisis & degraded-services — vetted repositories

The disaster-response knowledge bases the Crisis section draws from. All verified live
June 2026. The section is grounded in the Venezuelan reality — the **June 2026
Morón–Yumare earthquake** (24 June 2026, magnitude 7.2 then 7.5) on top of years of *apagones* (rolling blackouts
since the 2019 grid collapse), water rationing and medicine scarcity — but written to
serve any Fab City when services fail.

**Principle:** for water and health, figures are quoted verbatim from a primary authority
(WHO, CDC, EPA, Sphere) and never freelanced; every DIY health/water entry carries a
safety caveat. These methods reduce risk when services fail — they don't replace a working
utility or a clinician.

## Authorities — water, sanitation, health

- **WHO — Household Water Treatment & Safe Storage** — https://www.who.int/teams/environment-climate-change-and-health/water-sanitation-and-health/water-safety-and-quality/household-water-treatment-and-safe-storage — authoritative WASH guidance. Free.
- **CDC — Make Water Safe in an Emergency** — https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/index.html — boiling/chlorination/filtration with exact bleach tables. Free, public domain, Spanish available.
- **US EPA — Emergency Disinfection of Drinking Water** — https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/emergency-disinfection-drinking-water — bleach dosing by concentration + calcium-hypochlorite fallback. Free.
- **SODIS — EAWAG/Sandec** — https://www.eawag.ch/en/department/sandec/projects/swp/solar-water-disinfection-sodis/ — the authoritative solar water-disinfection method + manual. Free.
- **Hesperian Health Guides** — https://en.hesperian.org — *Where There Is No Doctor / No Dentist*, *Sanitation & Cleanliness* — community health where clinicians are absent. Free PDFs, 100+ languages.
- **The Sphere Handbook** — https://spherestandards.org — humanitarian minimum standards (the "how much is enough" reference, e.g. 15 L/person/day). Free.
- **IFRC / ICRC** — https://www.ifrc.org — Red Cross/Red Crescent field manuals (HWTS, epidemic control). Free PDFs.
- **American Red Cross — Prepare** — https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies.html — accessible household prep & water/food fact sheets (EN/ES). Free.

## Appropriate technology & humanitarian fabrication

- **Appropedia** — https://www.appropedia.org — largest open wiki for appropriate tech, water, energy, sanitation how-tos. Free, open.
- **Field Ready** — https://www.fieldready.org — humanitarian fabrication; locally-made/3D-printed relief & medical supplies. Open designs. (See OSHWA below.)
- **MIT D-Lab** — https://d-lab.mit.edu — co-designed appropriate tech for low-resource settings (cookstoves, energy). Free.
- **Public Lab** — https://publiclab.org — open/DIY community environmental monitoring (air, water). Free, open source.
- **Survivor Library** — https://www.survivorlibrary.com — archive of pre-industrial / low-tech manuals (water, medicine, fabrication). Free.
- **OSHWA certification directory — Field Ready** — https://certification.oshwa.org/list.html?q=field%20ready — 56 OSHWA-certified Field Ready designs (health consumables, WASH hardware). The 7 in the Crisis section are pulled from here with live cert UIDs (e.g. umbilical cord clamp US000203, oxygen fitting US000204, water pipe T-piece US000181). More to mine: IV bag hook US000205, ventilator connector US000211, nebulizer connector US000209, and the WA001–WA014 water-fitting family.

## Comms & coordination (off-grid / network-down)

- **Meshtastic** — https://meshtastic.org — off-grid encrypted text over long-range LoRa radio, no cell/internet. Open source (needs hardware, stage in advance).
- **Briar** — https://briarproject.org — phone-to-phone encrypted messaging over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi when networks are down. Open source.
- **Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT)** — https://www.hotosm.org — collaborative crisis mapping of buildings/roads. Free, open data.
- **Ushahidi** — https://www.ushahidi.com — crowdsourced incident/needs mapping (SMS/web) for coordinating aid. Free, open source.

## To verify before publishing specifics
Chronic 2026 fuel-queue and medicine-scarcity figures are widely reported but should be
confirmed against a fresh dated source — **ReliefWeb** (https://reliefweb.int), **UN OCHA**,
or **PAHO/WHO Venezuela** — before citing exact numbers.
