## One-pager (attach or paste)

### Things That Work — what it is
A growing, open repository of **solutions that work for people when services fail** — across water, energy, food, sanitation, health, shelter and fabrication. Three languages. Built for Fab City; the community-response layer of PLANETAI.

### What counts as a "thing that works"
Anything someone can **make, fix, repurpose or substitute** with what's at hand. Not theory — a thing that has been done. Examples already in the catalogue:

- A **tractor brake disc** re-cut from worn clutch fibre (Cuba, Special Period).
- A **solar still** that turns muddy or salt water drinkable with a bowl, a cup and plastic film (OLIVE, Japan).
- A **3D-printed oxygen-cylinder stand** for a field hospital with no trolley (MSF).
- A **biosand filter** that removes ~98% of pathogens by gravity (Appropedia).

If your lab has built, fixed, or improvised something that worked — it belongs.

### Two ways to add (10 minutes)
At **ttw.fab.city → Start**:
1. **Suggest a solution / a need** — propose something that works, *or* raise a problem your community faces that needs a solution. The network picks it up.
2. **Share a success** — a solution you've actually tested, here or anywhere, and what happened. Tested solutions are what the next family can trust.

Both land in the Fab City Foundation Airtable, get reviewed, and the good ones publish into the catalogue with your city credited.

### The pilots seed it; the network grows it
Barcelona · Boston · Santiago · Bali first (five each), then the wider 56-city Collective and the ~2,700 labs. Five each is all it takes to double the catalogue with grounded, place-specific knowledge.

### Honest note
It's a v0 prototype — your entries shape what it becomes. Nothing here pretends to be finished. That's the invitation.
